FGMLab

FGMLab is an open-source web appication for simulating and visualizing Fisher's geometric model. It allows students explore the model by providing interactive visualizations and animations of model dynamics. Fisher's geometric model is a model of trait evolution, and I created FGMlab to address the common conflation between the process of evolutionary change with its population genetic consequences (on which current evolution education focuses). The FGMLab application can be accessed at:
https://gabe-dubose.github.io/FGMLab/
